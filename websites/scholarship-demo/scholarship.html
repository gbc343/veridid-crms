About the CIBC Foundation

CIBC Foundation is the philanthropic arm of CIBC, one of Canada's largest banks. Founded in 1992, the Foundation has invested over $215M in community programs, with a particular focus on removing financial barriers to education. The Future Leaders Scholarship is the flagship program for post-secondary support.

About this scholarship

The Future Leaders Scholarship recognizes students who are the first in their immediate family to pursue post-secondary education, and who have demonstrated both academic strength and financial need. The award provides $10,000 paid directly to the student in USDC stablecoin — allowing recipients to use the funds for tuition, books, housing, or any genuine education-related cost.

Recipients are also invited to a two-day leadership retreat in Toronto each fall, and are matched with a CIBC mentor for the duration of their first academic year.

Eligibility requirements

  • Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or protected person
  • First in your immediate family to attend post-secondary education
  • Accepted or enrolled in a business, finance, accounting, or commerce program at an accredited Canadian institution
  • Minimum 80% (3.3 GPA) high-school average, or 3.0 GPA if currently enrolled
  • Demonstrated financial need (household income under $90,000)
  • Two letters of reference (one academic, one community)

How payments work

Once your application is approved, the award is released as $10,000 in USDC directly to the wallet you used to apply. There are no intermediaries and no tax withholding — Granted generates the necessary T4A documentation for your records. Average time from approval to funds-received: 12 seconds.
