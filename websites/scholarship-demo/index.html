Scholarships,
paid the moment you qualify.

Granted is a stablecoin-powered scholarship platform connecting students with foundations, universities, and corporate sponsors. Apply once with verified identity — funds are released on-chain directly to your wallet the day your application is approved.

Base eligibility: enrolled or accepted into an accredited post-secondary program · age 16+ · valid government ID. Individual scholarships may add field-of-study, regional, financial-need, or merit-based requirements.

  • Enrolled in accredited program
  • Verified identity (ID + selfie)
  • Minimum 2.5 GPA (program-dependent)
  • Compatible wallet (we'll help)
